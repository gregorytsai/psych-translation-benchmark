Scores, time, and sample size

How to read this model's measured numbers

The text model mean uses 29 complete shared cases: five AI reviewer scores are averaged within each case, then the case means are averaged. The image model mean uses 4 images with four reviewers per case. These are descriptive observations, not a human gold accuracy score; incomplete reviewer cells are not filled with zeros.

Warm-response medians measure response time after the model was loaded in the frozen translation run. They describe this sample and configuration, not the speed of every machine. P95 and sample size are shown above.
