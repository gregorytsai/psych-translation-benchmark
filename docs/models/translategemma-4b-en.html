How to read this model

Start with shared cases, then inspect outputs.

The text score uses 29 shared cases and five fixed reviewers; the image score uses four images and four fixed reviewers. These are observations, not a gold accuracy score, and incomplete panels are not filled with zeros.

The response medians come from the frozen translation run. They answer how long this configuration took after loading, not how fast every machine will be.

Risk example 02 · text-1851.600 score
English source excerpt

Panic disorder is a type of anxiety disorder. It causes repeated panic attacks, which are sudden periods of intense fear, discomfort, or a sense of losing control. These attacks happen even though there is no real danger. They often cause physical symptoms. For example, you may have a rapid or pounding heartbeat and feel like you are having a heart attack.

↓
Traditional Chinese output excerpt

焦慮症是一種焦慮障礙的一種。它會導致重複發生的焦慮發作，這些發作是突然的、強烈的恐懼、不適或感到失去控制的狀態。這些發作即使在沒有實際危險的情況下也會發生。它們通常會引起身體上的症狀。例如，您可能會感到心跳加速或劇烈，甚至感覺像要中風。

Why it matters

“Heart attack” became “stroke” (中風), confusing two different events. This is a directly checkable terminology error, not an estimate of the overall error rate.
