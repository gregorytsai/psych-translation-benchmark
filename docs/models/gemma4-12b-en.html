How to read this model

Start with shared cases, then inspect outputs.

The text score uses 29 shared cases and five fixed reviewers; the image score uses four images and four fixed reviewers. These are observations, not a gold accuracy score, and incomplete panels are not filled with zeros.

The response medians come from the frozen translation run. They answer how long this configuration took after loading, not how fast every machine will be.

Risk example 02 · text-2780.000 score
English source excerpt

Suicide doesn’t have just one cause, and it can’t be prevented with just one solution. Preventing suicide requires understanding and addressing the factors that increase risk for suicide and suicidal behaviors, helping those at higher risk, and supporting survivors of suicide loss.

↓
Traditional Chinese output excerpt

自殺並非只有一個原因，也無法僅用一個解決方案來預防。預防自殺需要了解並處理增加自殺和自殺行為風險的因素，幫助高風險對象，並支持自殺受難者。

Why it matters

“Survivors of suicide loss” refers to people bereaved by suicide. 自殺受難者 does not clearly retain that referent. Judges flagged a possible shift in who is being described.
