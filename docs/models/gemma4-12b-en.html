Scores, time, and sample size

How to read this model's measured numbers

The text model mean uses 29 complete shared cases: five AI reviewer scores are averaged within each case, then the case means are averaged. The image model mean uses 4 images with four reviewers per case. These are descriptive observations, not a human gold accuracy score; incomplete reviewer cells are not filled with zeros.

Warm-response medians measure response time after the model was loaded in the frozen translation run. They describe this sample and configuration, not the speed of every machine. P95 and sample size are shown above.

Risk example 02 · text-2780 score
English source excerpt

Suicide doesn’t have just one cause, and it can’t be prevented with just one solution. Preventing suicide requires understanding and addressing the factors that increase risk for suicide and suicidal behaviors, helping those at higher risk, and supporting survivors of suicide loss.

↓
Traditional Chinese output excerpt

自殺並非只有一個原因，也無法僅用一個解決方案來預防。預防自殺需要了解並處理增加自殺和自殺行為風險的因素，幫助高風險對象，並支持自殺受難者。

Why it matters

“Survivors of suicide loss” refers to people bereaved by suicide. 自殺受難者 does not clearly retain that referent. Judges flagged a possible shift in who is being described.
