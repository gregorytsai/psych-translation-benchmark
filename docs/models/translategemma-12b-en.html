How to read this model

Start with shared cases, then inspect outputs.

The text score uses 29 shared cases and five fixed reviewers; the image score uses four images and four fixed reviewers. These are observations, not a gold accuracy score, and incomplete panels are not filled with zeros.

The response medians come from the frozen translation run. They answer how long this configuration took after loading, not how fast every machine will be.

Risk example 02 · text-0888.000 score
English source excerpt

Bipolar depression is often treated with a mood stabilizer or an atypical antipsychotic. For some people experiencing a depressive episode, antidepressants may be added to these medications. However, antidepressants are not used alone because they can trigger a manic episode or rapid cycling in a person with bipolar disorder. Medications that target sleep or anxiety are sometimes added to mood stabilizers as part of a treatment plan.

↓
Traditional Chinese output excerpt

雙相情感障礙的憂鬱症通常會使用情緒穩定劑或非典型抗精神病藥物來治療。對於某些正在經歷憂鬱發作的人，醫生可能會在這些藥物之外，額外使用抗憂鬱藥物。然而，抗憂鬱藥物通常不會單獨使用，因為它們可能會引發躁狂發作或快速循環，這對患有雙相情感障礙的人來說是危險的。有時，針對睡眠或焦慮的藥物會被加入到情緒穩定劑的治療方案中。

Why it matters

The source says antidepressants “are not used alone”; the translation adds “usually,” softening the negation into a general tendency. This checks fidelity to the stimulus, not treatment advice.
