How to read this model

Start with shared cases, then inspect outputs.

The text score uses 29 shared cases and five fixed reviewers; the image score uses four images and four fixed reviewers. These are observations, not a gold accuracy score, and incomplete panels are not filled with zeros.

The response medians come from the frozen translation run. They answer how long this configuration took after loading, not how fast every machine will be.

English source excerpt

Psychotic symptoms include changes in the way a person thinks, acts, and experiences the world. A person experiencing psychotic symptoms often has disrupted thoughts and perceptions, and they may have difficulty recognizing what is real and what is not. For some people, these symptoms come and go. For others, the symptoms become stable over time. Psychotic symptoms include hallucinations, delusions, and thought disorder.

Traditional Chinese output excerpt

精神病症狀包括一個人思考、行動以及體驗世界方式的改變。經歷精神病症狀的人通常會有思考與感知中斷的情況，且他們可能難以分辨什麼是真實的，什麼不是。對某些人而言，這些症狀會反覆出現。對其他人而言，這些症狀隨著時間會變得穩定。精神病症狀包括幻覺、妄想以及思考障礙。

Why it matters

The source describes disrupted thoughts and perceptions; 中斷 can suggest that they stop. GPT flagged this possible meaning shift. A local concern can coexist with a high overall mean and still warrants expert review.
