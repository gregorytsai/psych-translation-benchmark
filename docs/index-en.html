How LLMs scored the translations

Five LLM judges (Qwen, Nemotron, Mistral, GPT and Gemini) compared each translation with the English source without seeing the model name. The text score averages the five judges for each passage, then averages the 29 shared passages. Four judges scored all four images. Mistral did not support images in this setup.

30 · Terminology and Taiwan usageClinical terms, character forms and local usage.
30 · Meaning and safetyPreservation of negation, drug names, doses, numbers and conditions.
20 · FluencyClarity and natural wording in Taiwan Traditional Chinese.
20 · Completeness and formatOmissions, additions and changes to lists or visible structure.
On text, the intraclass correlation coefficient (ICC) was 0.58 for one judge and 0.87 for the average of five. A higher ICC indicates more consistent ratings. The 100-point total summarizes these four criteria. A score of 98 does not imply that 98% of a translation is correct.

The translations did not undergo item-by-item human review or scoring.

Limits of this run

The text comparison covers 29 passages from 12 source pages. The image comparison covers four layouts. Timing uses all 30 text requests and all four image requests per model, on one hardware and software configuration. The file sizes are disk space occupied by the model and its vision projector. Peak RAM was not measured.

Methods and test settings

Drug-name transcription on a synthetic label

In img-04, Gemma 4 26B transcribed SERTRALINE as SETRALINE, dropping the first R. All four image judges flagged the mismatch. Their mean score for this case was 72 out of 100.

General-purpose and translation-specialized models on text
